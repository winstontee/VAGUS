# Current site: vaguseffect.com

Reviewed 2026-10-28. Squarespace. Pages: Home, About, Services, Blog,
Contact, Discovery Call, Privacy Policy.

## What's there

- **Headline:** "Trauma-Informed Counselling — Grounded in Nervous System
  Awareness."
- **Who:** Amber Shanahan, counsellor in Vancouver, BC, offering online
  counselling across Canada.
- **Credentials (About page):** RTCc with ACCT; working toward CPCA MPCC-P
  with supervised hours in progress; Registered Holistic Nutritionist; somatic
  practitioner, somatic EMDR, vagal toning, trauma-informed coach and other
  certificates.
- **Her story (About page):** ongoing health challenges, then her son's
  prolonged hospitalisation after medical errors, led her back to school in
  midlife — nutrition first, then the nervous system, trauma and counselling.
  This is her published story; use it only in her own words.
- **Who it's for (Services):** people on edge or overwhelmed, shutting down
  or numbing, big emotional reactions, unsafe-feeling relationships, harsh
  inner critic or people-pleasing. Mentions complex trauma / CPTSD gently,
  without requiring a label or diagnosis.
- **Offer:** individual online counselling; free 10-minute discovery call
  via a form, with an email reply in 1–2 business days.
- **Blog:** 4 posts (somatic therapy x2, high-functioning anxiety, vagus
  nerve tips).
- **Social:** Instagram @thevaguseffect.

## Voice

Calm, warm, unhurried, non-pathologising. Short reflective sentences. Key
ideas: patterns "developed for a reason"; curiosity not judgement; "at a pace
that feels right for you"; working *with* the nervous system. This is already
strong and is the reference for all drafts. Exception: the older "Superhero
of Your Own Body" blog post is in a jokey style that doesn't match.

## Gaps (where clients are being lost)

1. **No email capture.** Visitors who aren't ready to book leave with nothing.
   No lead magnet or newsletter.
2. **Booking friction.** A form, then waiting 1–2 days for an email. No
   self-booking calendar.
3. **No pricing or fees info.** Not on Services. People in this audience
   often won't enquire without knowing cost.
4. **No mention of the program** or any next step beyond 1:1.
5. **Thin content for search.** 4 blog posts; nothing targeting what people
   type (e.g. "CPTSD counsellor Vancouver", "somatic counselling online BC").
6. **Directory presence unknown.** No Psychology Today or ACCT directory link.
7. **Minor SEO:** the Discovery Call page's title is "Appointments 3".
8. **Instagram** links point to posts from early 2024 — check whether it's
   still active.
