# Audience: who we're talking to

Status: **draft v1, 2026-10-28.** Read with `positioning.md`.

This is a picture of the *ideal client*, built from her Services page,
published descriptions of complex trauma, and public peer-forum language.
**It is not based on her actual clients**, and must never be. CLAUDE.md
rules out client information in this public repo. Once she has a few months
of 1:1 work, she can tell us in general terms which parts ring true, and
we'll revise. Guesses are marked *(guess)*.

## 1. What they're struggling with

Her Services page lists the core experiences (see
`docs/research/current-site.md`). Published frameworks support the same
picture:

| Experience | How it shows up day to day | Support |
|---|---|---|
| **On edge / overwhelmed** | Can't switch off. Startles easily, braced for the next thing, sleep is light. | Her Services page. ICD-11 "affect dysregulation" cluster ([Brewin 2020](https://www.cambridge.org:443/core/services/aop-cambridge-core/content/view/E53B8CD7CF9B725FE651720EE58E93A4/S0007125020000434a.pdf/icd11_complex_posttraumatic_stress_disorder_simplifying_diagnosis_in_trauma_populations.pdf)) |
| **Shut down / numb** | Flat, foggy, "going through the motions". Disappears into the phone or into work. Hard to feel anything. | Her Services page. Freeze/shutdown as described in [Pete Walker's 4F model (summary)](https://anniewright.com/pete-walker-complex-ptsd-four-fs-fawn-response-guide/) |
| **Big reactions that feel out of proportion** | Sudden waves of shame, panic or despair, often set off by small things. Afterwards: "Why did I react like that?" | Her Services page. Walker's "emotional flashbacks" ([pete-walker.com](https://pete-walker.com/flashbackManagement.htm)) |
| **Harsh inner critic** | Constant self-judgement, perfectionism, never good enough. | Her Services page. [Walker – Shrinking the Inner Critic](https://www.pete-walker.com/shrinkingInnerCritic.htm). ICD-11 "negative self-concept" |
| **People-pleasing / fawning** | Can't say no, reads every mood in the room, loses track of what she wants. | Her Services page. Walker's "fawn" response ([summary](https://anniewright.com/fawn-response/)) |
| **Relationships feel unsafe or confusing** | Pulls away or clings, repeats patterns, feels alone even with others. | Her Services page. ICD-11 "disturbances in relationships" |
| **Body carries it** | Tension, fatigue, gut trouble, feeling disconnected from the body. | *(guess)*. Common in somatic-practice framing. She'd know from her nutrition work. Don't make medical claims. |
| **High-functioning on the outside** | Holds a job and a household together, so nobody, including her, thinks it "counts". | Her blog post on high-functioning anxiety. *(guess on prevalence)* |

**Life context** *(guess)*: often 30s to 50s, often caregivers, often
looking for help after a life event (a breakup, burnout, a parent's illness,
becoming a parent) that brought old patterns to the surface.

### 2b. Forum and peer language (how they talk to each other)

Seen in public peer spaces (r/CPTSD, CPTSD Foundation, Walker's readers).
Use it to *recognise* the reader, never to diagnose her.

- "emotional flashback", "I got flashbacked"
  ([Walker](https://pete-walker.com/flashbackManagement.htm))
- "fawning", "I fawn", "people-pleaser"
  ([summary](https://anniewright.com/fawn-response/))
- "freeze", "shutdown", "frozen at work", "how do I thaw"
  ([r/CPTSD threads via mirror, 2026](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1s7at43/snapshots/2026-04-03T22%3A11%3A33.894393Z))
- "dysregulated", "my nervous system is fried"
  ([r/CPTSD via mirror](https://reddit.sentinel-team.org/posts/1tw6w3x/snapshots/2026-06-05T17%3A01%3A01.624652Z))
- "inner critic", "the critic"
  ([CPTSD Foundation](https://cptsdfoundation.org/2019/07/08/the-connection-between-emotional-flashbacks-and-the-inner-critic/))
- "hypervigilant", "walking on eggshells", "too much / not enough",
  "I'm fine, but…", "nothing *that* bad happened to me" *(guess, common
  phrasing. Not verified in a counted sample)*
- "functional freeze" *(guess. A popular social-media phrase, but we found
  no source for it)*

**Use with care:** this audience is often well-read, and they can tell when
someone copies their language without understanding it. One accurate phrase
is better than five buzzwords. Amber should keep only the phrases she
actually hears and uses.

## 3. What stops them booking

Ranked by how much we think each one costs her. Fixes point to
`offer-ladder.md` and `channels.md`.

| Barrier | Evidence | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **Cost, and not knowing the cost** | Mental health care sits largely outside public coverage in Canada, and 15% of Canadians reported cost stopping them getting mental health care (2023 Commonwealth Fund survey, [CIHI](https://www.cihi.ca/en/canadians-report-increasing-need-for-mental-health-care-alongside-barriers-to-access)). 1 in 3 people with mental health care needs reported them unmet or only partly met ([StatCan 2023](https://publications.gc.ca/collections/collection_2023/statcan/75-006-x/75-006-2023-11-eng.pdf)). Her site has no fees info (current-site.md gap 3). | Publish a fees page with the candidate rate and sliding-scale spots |
| **Can't use insurance** | Coverage found lists full ACCT designations, not candidates (CLAUDE.md). | Say so plainly on the fees page. Present the candidate rate as the reason it's affordable without insurance. |
| **Waiting and friction** | Form, then a 1 to 2 day email reply (current-site.md gap 2). People in a fawn or freeze state are likely to give up on multi-step contact *(guess)*. | Self-booking for discovery calls |
| **"It's not bad enough" / "I don't have a diagnosis"** | Her "no label needed" framing already speaks to this. | Lead with it (positioning A/C) |
| **Fear of having to retell everything** | *(guess, common in trauma-informed practice copy)* | Say clearly: "You don't have to tell your story to start. We go at your pace." Only if this matches how she works. [PLACEHOLDER: confirm] |
| **Not sure what "RTCc" means, or whether she's qualified** | The title is unfamiliar to most people *(guess)*. | A short, plain FAQ: what an RTC Candidate is, ACCT, supervision [PLACEHOLDER: supervision disclosure], training list |
| **Past bad experiences with counselling** | *(guess)* | Relational framing: "you can tell me if something isn't working" |
| **Online feels impersonal** | *(guess)* | Short explainer: what an online session looks like, privacy, being in your own space |
| **Not ready yet** | No email capture, so they leave with nothing (current-site.md gap 1). | Lead magnet + email list (offer-ladder rung 0) |
