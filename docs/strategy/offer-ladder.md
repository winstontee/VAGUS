## Rung 0: Free lead magnet + email list

**Purpose:** catch visitors who aren't ready to book (current-site.md gap 1)
and build the list the program will later be offered to.

**Recommended:** a short, printable **"Noticing your nervous system" guide**:
- A one-page plain-language map of "on edge", "shut down" and "settled"
  states. Experience words, no anatomy claims (see the polyvagal note in
  `positioning.md`).
- Three or four grounding and orienting practices she already teaches.
  [PLACEHOLDER: which practices she uses and is comfortable sharing]
- "If this sounds familiar" framing, with a gentle pointer to a
  professional assessment and to a discovery call.
- **Crisis box on page 1:** call or text 9-8-8, or 911 in an emergency
  ([Government of Canada](https://www.canada.ca/en/public-health/news/2023/11/9-8-8-suicide-crisis-helpline.html)).
  Verify at [988.ca](https://988.ca) before publishing.

**Don't build:** a symptom quiz or "do you have C-PTSD?" checklist. It reads
as screening or diagnosis, which CLAUDE.md forbids. No trauma-processing
exercises, journaling prompts about memories, or "inner child" scripts.
Regulation and grounding only.

**Follow-up:** a 4 to 5 email welcome sequence (content-writer owns this).
The last email invites a discovery call.

**Tool:** [PLACEHOLDER: email tool, e.g. Squarespace Email Campaigns or
MailerLite. Not yet provided.] Squarespace's native forms may be the
lowest-friction option since the site is on Squarespace *(guess; she should
check which plan she's on)*.

**Price:** free.

### Recommended range (reasoned guess)

> **Standard candidate rate: $95–$120 per 50-minute session.**
> **Plus 2–4 sliding-scale spots at $60–$85.**

Reasoning:

1. **Above intern rates.** She's a registered candidate with a professional
   association, with substantial prior training (RHN, somatic, somatic EMDR,
   coaching), not a practicum student. Agency intern rates of ~$45–$85 are
   the floor.
2. **Clearly below RCC rates.** RCCs post $135–$170+, and BCACC's guidance
   runs higher. Her candidate status should be visible in the price as well
   as the title. Pricing near RCC rates while not covered by insurance would
   make her the most expensive option *to the client*.
3. **No insurance changes the comparison.** A client whose extended health
   plan reimburses an RCC pays less out of pocket for the RCC than a $150
   uninsured session with her. *(Guess: typical reimbursement percentages
   and annual maximums vary by plan; we didn't source them.)* Her real
   competitors on price are agency interns with waitlists of 1 to 2 months
   ([Langara list](https://students.langara.ca/student-services/counselling/pdf/reduced-cost-counselling-resources-final.pdf))
   and uninsured clients choosing between her and an RCC.
4. **It's close to the "final stages of training" tier** one BC practice
   uses ($100), which is the nearest published comparison to an RTCc.
5. **Sliding-scale spots** match the norm (72% of BCACC members) and suit
   an audience where cost is the top barrier (`audience.md`). Capping the
   number of spots protects her income.

Where in the range: closer to $95–$105 if speed to first clients is the
priority, closer to $115–$120 if she already has steady enquiries.
*(Guess.)*

### Other notes for rung 2

- **Insurance:** state plainly that RTCc sessions aren't currently covered
  by extended health plans. Pacific Blue Cross direct billing lists ACCT as
  an association
  ([PBC provider news](https://pac.bluecross.ca/providerresource/provider-news/direct-billing-for-mental-health-providers-in-bc-starting-july-11/)),
  but the coverage we found names full designations (RTC/MTC/RCS), not
  candidates (CLAUDE.md). Don't imply coverage. Revisit when she reaches
  full RTC.
- **GST:** psychotherapy and counselling therapy became GST/HST-exempt in
  2024 for qualifying practitioners
  ([CRA tax tip](https://www.canada.ca/en/revenue-agency/news/newsroom/tax-tips/tax-tips-2024/clarifying-new-gsthst-exemption-psychotherapy-counselling-therapy-services.html?rss)),
  and BC RCCs were confirmed exempt in December 2024
  ([newswire](https://newswire.ca/news-releases/british-columbia-s-registered-clinical-counsellors-rccs-now-exempt-from-charging-gst-for-psychotherapy-services-888482321.html)).
  **Whether this covers an RTCc is unknown.** It's a question for her
  accountant, not something to state in copy.
  [PLACEHOLDER: does she charge GST?]
- **Price increases:** tell clients up front that the candidate rate will
  change when she reaches full RTC, with notice to existing clients.
