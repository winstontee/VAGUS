## 2. Counsellor-to-counsellor referrals

Other counsellors turn people away every week: full caseloads, clients who
can't afford RCC rates, or clients whose needs fall outside their focus.
Vancouver's low-cost agency options run 1 to 2 month waitlists
([Langara reduced-cost list](https://students.langara.ca/student-services/counselling/pdf/reduced-cost-counselling-resources-final.pdf)).
A candidate-rate counsellor with openings this month solves a problem for
them.

Who to approach:
- Her ACCT cohort, training program and supervision peers. Ask who has a
  waitlist.
- Full RCC practices in Vancouver that focus on trauma or somatic work.
- Intake coordinators at group practices and low-cost clinics.

What to send: a short note (content-writer can draft a template) saying
who she works with, her candidate rate and sliding-scale spots, current
openings, and how to book. Offer a 15-minute call.

Check with the ethics reviewer: no referral fees or kickbacks, and how
referrals are handled under the ACCT Code. [PLACEHOLDER: ACCT Code copy
from Amber]

## 5. Google Business Profile: probably not eligible

Google's guidelines exclude **online-only businesses**. A profile needs
in-person contact with customers at a location or within a service area
([Search Engine Land summary of Google guidelines](https://searchengineland.com/how-to-follow-google-business-profile-guidelines-432177);
[Google Business Profile community answer](https://support.google.com/business/thread/169106319/how-do-i-list-my-business-location-on-google-when-i-work-from-home-and-also-offer-on-line-services?hl=en)).
Creating one for an online-only practice risks suspension.

- **If she only works online, skip GBP.** Local signals instead come from
  consistent directory listings and BC/Vancouver wording on the site.
- **If she sees anyone in person** (an office, a rented room, walk-and-talk
  sessions) she may qualify. Revisit then.
  [PLACEHOLDER: does she offer any in-person sessions, and where?]
